Working With Images

The PNG version of the image is 80 KB while the JPG version is 82 KB. JPG is the larger file because it's a simple picture with flat areas of color which PNG's lossless compression can deal with more efficiently. Looking at the two side by side, there isn't much of a difference besides the black border which surrounds the JPG.
